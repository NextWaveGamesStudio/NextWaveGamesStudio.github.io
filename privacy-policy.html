Privacy Policy for PuzzleBloom

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PuzzleBloom, an Android game published by NextWave Games Studio. PuzzleBloom is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Information you provide

PuzzleBloom does not require you to create an account. We do not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other personally identifying information through the game.

Advertising and automatically processed data

PuzzleBloom uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information; and
  • Device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Google handles information according to its own policies. You can learn more through the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information for apps that use its services.

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, users may be shown a consent message that allows them to accept, reject, or manage the use of their data for advertising. Depending on a user's location, device settings, and consent choice, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may contact us using the email address below for help with their privacy choices. A Privacy Options control will also be made available inside the game where required, allowing users to review or change an earlier consent decision.

Users under 13

PuzzleBloom is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Data security and retention

Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption. Data processed by Google is retained and protected according to Google's policies and the user's settings. Because we do not operate user accounts or directly collect personal information through PuzzleBloom, we do not maintain a separate player-personal-data database for the game.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your Android device settings. You may also contact us with questions about this Privacy Policy or your privacy choices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PuzzleBloom's features, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
